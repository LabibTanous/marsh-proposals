01

Social media management

What we do

  • We set up your company's pages on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat, and launch them.
  • We upload your company's products to every platform.
  • We publish content according to the content strategy.

What you get

  • Pages ready on the platforms you choose.
  • Your products listed on every platform.
02

Content strategy

What we do

  • We write a content strategy for your business, covering post ideas, filming and design.
  • We build it around your target audience.
  • We review and adjust the marketing strategy with you every 45 days.

What you get

  • A content strategy for your business.
  • A review of the marketing strategy every 45 days.
03

Filming and video

What we do

  • We film at least 3 videos a month.
  • We re-edit videos adapted from outside content to fit your account.

What you get

  • At least 3 new videos each month. Extra videos cost extra.
  • Videos and designs ready to post.
04

Design and visual identity

What we do

  • We build the visual identity of your brand.
  • We design every visual element of your posts.

What you get

  • One consistent visual identity across your posts.
  • A design for every post.
05

Paid ads

What we do

  • We plan ad campaigns and manage their funding on every platform.
  • Ads are paid from a bank card issued in your company's name.

What you get

  • The ad budget stays on your company's card.
  • A report with reach, messages, and the cost per message.
06

Reports and follow-up

What we do

  • We send a performance report every week, with competitor analysis and a comparison of results.
  • We visit your company every 45 days.
  • We meet with you every 45 days to review and adjust the marketing strategy.

What you get

  • A weekly performance report.
  • A site visit and a meeting every 45 days.
07

Staff training

What we do

  • We train the employee who answers calls and messages, and supervise their work.
  • We train a spokesperson to present content in a way that suits your audience.

What you get

  • An employee who answers customers in the style and tone we trained them in.
  • A spokesperson who presents your content.
08

Websites

What we do

  • We design, build and launch your company's website.

What you get

  • A website for your business.
09

Marketing workflows

What we do

  • We use one system for content production, video, design, working with you, and publishing to social media.
10

Work review and automation

What we do

  • We interview each person on your team about how they do their work each day. They answer by voice note or text, in Arabic or English.
  • We read the answers together with your files, your messages and your meeting notes.
  • We list the tasks that repeat, the information each task needs, and who does it.
  • We mark the tasks that are worth automating, and agree the order with you.
  • We build the automations you choose, with your team.

What you get

  • A map of your team's tasks.
  • A list of the tasks to automate, in order.
  • The automations you choose, set up and in use.
