How we work

These are the steps of the monthly account management package. We agree the details with each business in its price offer.

What we do

At the start
  • We set up your pages on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat, and your website, and launch them.
  • We build the visual identity of your brand.
  • We write a content strategy: post ideas, filming and design.
  • We upload your company's products to every platform.
Every month
  • We film at least 3 videos. Extra videos cost extra.
  • We design every visual element of your posts.
  • We re-edit videos adapted from outside content.
  • We run paid ad campaigns on every platform.
Every week
  • We send a performance report, with competitor analysis and a comparison of results.
Every 45 days
  • We meet with you to review and adjust the marketing strategy.
  • We visit your company.
Throughout
  • We train the employee who answers calls and messages, and supervise their work.
  • We train a spokesperson to present content.

What we need from you

Your company does the following:

  1. 1

    Answer customers in the style and tone the Marsh team trains you in.

  2. 2

    Send a detailed sales and marketing report every 3 days.

  3. 3

    Attend a meeting every 45 days to review the marketing strategy.

  4. 4

    Suggest which products to promote, by season and market trend.

  5. 5

    Share competitors' latest offers and discounts.

  6. 6

    Tell us your company's strengths.

  7. 7

    Issue a bank card in your company's name to fund the ads.

Notes

  • Ads are funded only from your company's card.
  • Any video outside the agreement costs extra, set by its type and needs.

Ask for a price offer

Tell us about your business and what it needs. We send you a price offer that lists the services.
